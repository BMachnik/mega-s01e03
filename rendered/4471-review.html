What we report

Parser. The shared parser reads dates month first, while day-first tenants write the day first. This can swap the intended day and month. Assignment requires a day-first tenant and an app/email report or a booking-v2 form report. App/email reports from booking-v3 tenants also belong here; the form-hint condition below applies only to the form channel.

Parser issue: shared month-first date interpretation

Hint. Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY, while its form interprets the date in the tenant’s day-first order. Following the displayed hint can therefore produce the wrong date. Assignment requires a day-first tenant, the form channel and booking-v3’s month-first hint. The hint and the form’s interpretation need to agree.

Hint issue: booking-v3 date guidance and interpretation disagree

Intake tags are guesses based on the reporter’s browser. The table shows tags within each computed cause, including disagreements. Tenant locale, tenant form and report channel determine assignment; subject, browser locale and intake tag do not.

The desk-note reports footnoted under the table remain counted once in the parser group. Their wording includes one-day differences, a June-only shift and an invite arriving before booking. Those symptoms need further investigation: they do not establish a timezone defect or another cause. The export provides classification evidence, not a verified production fix.

Incoming reports and merges

The received series groups reports by receivedAt, when they reached the desk. The merged series groups them by mergedAt, when they were attached to the ticket. A merge spike can collect older reports; it is not evidence of a matching spike in new arrivals. For example, R-41200 was received on 2026-04-01 and merged on 2026-07-02.

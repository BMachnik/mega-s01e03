# Booking-v3 date hint conflicts with day-first interpretation — 33 reports

## Problem and assignment

The exported KB describes booking-v3 displaying **MM/DD/YYYY** while its form reads dates in the tenant's day-first order. A user following that hint can therefore submit a date interpreted with day and month reversed. Expected behavior is that the displayed date hint agrees with the form's interpretation.

The executable assignment in `src/lib/kb/causes.ts:40` requires an existing day-first tenant, channel `form`, and a month-first form hint. `DATE_HINT` in `src/lib/kb/causes.ts:24` maps booking-v3 to MM/DD/YYYY and booking-v2 to DD/MM/YYYY. App/email reports from booking-v3 tenants and booking-v2 form reports are assigned to the separate parser group.

## Evidence and scope

`delivery/analysis.json`, generated by `node scripts/analyze.mjs --write` from the original classifier and exported data, assigns **33 unique reports across 3 tenants** to this group: Alder Physio (de-DE), Copper Studio (es-ES) and Pale Harbour (pt-PT), all configured for booking-v3. Its received-date range is **2026-04-17–2026-09-22**. The complete ID set is in the `hint` group's `ids` array.

Representative reports from `src/lib/data/reports-4471.json` are:

- **R-41230**, Copper Studio, form: a client came on 1 June instead of 6 January.
- **R-41241**, Alder Physio, form: people arrived on 8 June for bookings intended for 6 August.
- **R-41251**, Pale Harbour, form: 7 January appeared as 1 July.

The 33 hint IDs and 379 parser IDs are disjoint and account for all 412 unique reports in #4471; the 427 exported rows include 15 repeats. Intake tags would give the wrong split: 15 hint-group reports, including R-41230 and R-41251, are tagged `parser`. Subject and browser locale also do not drive the classifier.

Receipt (`receivedAt`) and merging (`mergedAt`) are separate events. R-41230 arrived on 2026-04-17 and was merged on 2026-07-02; the merge date is not its arrival date.

## Limits

The five unusual desk-note reports belong to the parser group's footnote, not this group or a third cause. This finding describes the exported KB's classification and reported symptoms; the export supplies neither the production form implementation nor a verified fix. Tenant configuration is in `src/lib/data/tenants/`; the mechanism is described beside the executable assignment in `src/lib/kb/causes.ts:37`.
